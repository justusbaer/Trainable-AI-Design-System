Subpixel Rendering & Font Weight Fidelity

Diagnosis: -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased enforces grayscale smoothing on macOS, artificially thinning strokes and causing fonts to look 100-200 weight units lighter than authentic Google News.

Fix: Setting -webkit-font-smoothing: auto restores native subpixel antialiasing with genuine stroke boldness, exactly replicating live Google News.
